# Code of Conduct

*Last updated: Q1 2026*

## Our Standards

Noviscia is a community of builders, traders, and DeFi enthusiasts. We expect
everyone participating in the forum, governance, Discord, Telegram, or any
Noviscia channel to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Treat differing opinions with curiosity, not hostility.
- Keep proposal threads focused on reasoning, math, and on-chain state.
- Credit others' ideas and research.
- Help newcomers understand the protocol.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, doxxing, or intimidation of any kind.
- Price talk or market manipulation talk in proposal threads.
- Spamming, flooding, or off-topic noise in governance discussions.
- Doxxing wallet addresses to pressure or threaten others.
- Impersonating team members, moderators, or other community members.
- Sharing malicious links or attempting to phish wallet keys.

## Governance Etiquette

1. One idea per heatcheck.
2. Cite math or on-chain state where possible.
3. No personal attacks — attack arguments, never people.
4. Respect the status markers: Discussion → Heatcheck → Snapshot → Adopted.

## Reporting

Report violations privately to moderators on Discord or by messaging
the admissions council through the forum. Reports are reviewed
confidentially and without retaliation.

Moderators may remove content, lock threads, or suspend accounts for
repeat violations. On-chain governance activity is governed by the
staking contract's own rules — this Code of Conduct applies to
discussion channels.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all public Noviscia spaces, including the
forum (Noviscia Research), governance feed, Discord, Telegram, Twitter
replies, and GitHub Discussions.

Violations in public channels are addressed by community moderators.
Adopted proposals and on-chain execution are governed by the clearing
house's governance program.
